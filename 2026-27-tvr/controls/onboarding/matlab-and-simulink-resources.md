# Project-Specific Toolbox Documentation

**Control System Toolbox:** https://www.mathworks.com/products/control.html 

- Used for designing and simulating control systems 

**Simscape Multibody:** https://www.mathworks.com/help/simscape/index.html?s_tid=CRUX_topnav  

- Applying forces and joints to mechanical systems

**System Identification:** https://www.mathworks.com/help/ident/ug/using-system-identification-toolbox-blocks-in-simulink-models.html  

- Develop transfer functions or state-estimation models based on input/output data sets

**Aerospace:** https://www.mathworks.com/help/aerotbx/index.html?s_tid=CRUX_topnav  

- Model aerospace objects and behaviour
